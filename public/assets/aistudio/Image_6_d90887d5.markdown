## Brand & Style
This design system embodies an immersive, futuristic command-and-control aesthetic fused with tactical utility. Built for high-density monitoring environments, performance tooling, and immersive computational dashboards, it balances an abyssal midnight-violet canvas with sharp, industrial olive structures and high-voltage kinetic accents.

The visual style combines modern tactical minimalism with purposeful luminescence. It rejects gratuitous decoration in favor of ultra-high-contrast legibility, focused information hierarchies, and surgical signal identification. Deep obsidian voids push noise into the background, while piercing neon orange and acidic lime bring critical operations directly into focus.

## Colors
The palette leverages chromatic priority to establish instantaneous visual triage:

- **Deep Obsidian & Midnight Violet**: Layered base surfaces (`#0d0914`, `#150f22`, and `#1f1633`) create depth without stark, pitch-black starkness. They preserve ocular comfort during sustained exposure.
- **Tactical Olive Drab**: Secondary grounding tones (`#2a2e1d` and `#3c422a`) construct subdued outlines, structured metadata badges, and low-priority system containers.
- **Electric Neon Orange**: Primary focus accent (`#ff6200` to `#ff7700`) allocated strictly for high-visibility triggers, active state indicators, critical path operations, and priority system alerts.
- **Acidic Lime / Neon Yellow**: Precision metric indicators (`#e2f705` and `#ffe600`) targeted for telemetry values, progress bars, dynamic status badges, and rapid visual confirmation.
- **Text & Foreground**: Crisp cool-tinted whites (`#f3f1f7`) for primary labels, stepping down to muted lilac-ash tones (`#9b92aa` and `#625973`) for secondary and tertiary copy.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on the structural precision of `Inter` for interfaces and headlines, paired alongside `JetBrains Mono` for metadata, readouts, labels, and analytical metrics. 

- Maintain tight letter-spacing on headlines to generate punchy, condensed titles.
- Use mono-spaced labels in uppercase to evoke instrument panels and telemetry monitors.
- Maintain high contrast between primary information layers (`#f3f1f7`) and secondary contextual text (`#9b92aa`). Avoid low-contrast text on critical metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
Layouts use an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for dense information distribution without visual crowding.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, 1rem margin, 1rem gutter. Structural elements stack vertically; metric strips overflow via horizontal pan.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout, 2rem margin, 1.5rem gutter. Split panels activate for side-by-side analysis.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout, 3rem margin, 2rem gutter. High-density data matrices, persistent side rails, and multi-pane control surfaces operate concurrently.
- Maintain consistent internal component padding using the base 4px/8px modular scale (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to anchor technical precision across all screens.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through subtle chromatic stepping and ambient volumetric glows rather than traditional drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiering**:
  - `Base Floor`: Midnight Obsidian (`#0d0914`).
  - `Surface 1 (Panels & Shells)`: Deep Violet (`#150f22`).
  - `Surface 2 (Cards & Active Modules)`: Elevated Violet Container (`#1f1633`).
- **Tactical Borders**: Boundaries rely on structural 1px hairline rules using dark olive tones (`#2a2e1d` and `#3c422a`) rather than dropshadow cutoffs.
- **Glow & Bloom**: Interactive hot-states apply a controlled, localized photon glow (`0 0 16px rgba(255, 98, 0, 0.28)` for orange; `0 0 16px rgba(226, 247, 5, 0.24)` for lime) to simulate active terminal hardware.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, utilitarian, and firm. Sharp 4px corners (`roundedness: 1`) govern buttons, cards, containers, and inputs, rejecting fluid bulbous contours in favor of an engineered hardware chassis profile.

- Default containers and inputs use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Larger modal surfaces and floating overlays utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges and status pills retain clean, compact boundaries without full stadium curvature to sustain the tactical identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#ff6200`, text `#0d0914`, bold mono font. On hover, background shifts to `#ff7700` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(255, 98, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Tactical**: Background `#1f1633`, border `1px solid #3c422a`, text `#f3f1f7`. On hover, border illuminates to `#e2f705` and text brightens.
- **Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#9b92aa`. On hover, text turns `#ff6200` with subtle `#1f1633` background fill.

### Metric Indicators & Chips
- **Status Badges**: Semi-translucent dark olive background (`rgba(42, 46, 29, 0.6)`), border `1px solid #3c422a`, monospaced typography.
- **Telemetry Indicators**: For key performance readings, use neon lime (`#e2f705`) text accents alongside miniature 6px indicator dots pulsing with matching box-shadow.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Base: Surface fill `#150f22`, structural border `1px solid #2a2e1d`, text `#f3f1f7`, placeholder `#625973`.
- Active / Focused: Border switches crisply to `#ff6200`, accompanied by an outer hairline ring (`0 0 0 1px #ff6200`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Unselected: Square frame with 2px radius, `#1f1633` fill, `#3c422a` border.
- Selected: Background transitions to `#ff6200` with dark obsidian `#0d0914` check icon, surrounded by an active glow.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Surface `#150f22` with a 1px exterior rim of `#2a2e1d`. 
- High-priority cards feature an anchored 2px neon accent along their top or leading border in either `#ff6200` or `#e2f705` to denote alert states or critical path items.

### Data Tables & Feed Lists
- Alternating rows using `#150f22` and `#1a1329`. Row borders defined by hairline `#2a2e1d`.
- Active/selected rows highlight with a vertical leading indicator in `#e2f705` and subtle full-row tinting.
