## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for deep work, technical orchestration, and sustained cognitive focus. Designed specifically for power users, engineers, product architects, and researchers, the interface minimizes ocular strain across marathon sessions while projecting quiet authority and bespoke craftsmanship.

The visual direction combines **Chiaroscuro Minimalism** with **Tonal Structuralism**:
- **Canvas Atmosphere:** Deep, warm obsidian-purple depths replace harsh OLED blacks or cold blue greys, establishing a restful, cinematic backdrop.
- **Architectural Division:** Structural hierarchy is achieved through muted moss-olive groupings and precise tonal shifts rather than stark grid lines.
